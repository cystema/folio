## Design Context

### Users
The site is primarily for hiring managers, founders, and technical peers evaluating Shubham quickly. They should leave with the sense that he can build serious production AI systems and has high agency. Writing should support that impression, but not dominate the identity of the site.

### Brand Personality
The site should feel like a builder/operator with quietly premium execution: direct, evidence-minded, restrained, and serious. The tone should avoid marketing language and avoid performing taste too loudly.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-only, but not neon, glossy, or "AI engineer" themed. The selected direction is Personal Archive with a Charcoal Paper treatment: warm black, parchment-like text, quiet spacing, and archive-like structure. It should feel subtle, indexed, and text-first rather than like a conventional hero or portfolio.

### Design Principles
- Use a masthead, not a marketing hero: name, current work, thesis, and links should be visible immediately without headline theater.
- Prioritize quiet credibility over broad completeness: no project grids, tech-stack walls, generic SaaS sections, academic CV layout, or anonymous minimal blog styling.
- Treat Field Notes honestly: show a restrained empty state, "No public notes yet.", until real writing exists.
- Keep the link surface small and purposeful: Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, and RSS.
- Make the first impression operational: "Founding engineer focused on the operational layer of AI products."
- Preserve the existing FaceTracker as a small rail signature in the masthead, not as a large hero gimmick.
- Use Fraunces for the masthead thesis and Instrument Sans for supporting/interface text. Avoid IBM Plex Mono as a primary type direction for this site.
